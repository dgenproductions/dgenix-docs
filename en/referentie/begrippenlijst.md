# Glossary

Every term used in dGENIX and in this documentation, alphabetically, with one sentence per term that reads on its own.

## A

**AI Content Engine** , the engine that turns a long video into short clips with
a virality score, plus titles, captions and hashtags. Available from Pro.
See [Growth Tools](https://dgenix.com/engines/ai-content).

**Assistant** , see *GENI*.

**Authority Engine** , the engine that builds a topic plan and writes the
articles to match, so you become authoritative on a whole theme instead of a
single keyword. Available from Pro.

**Automation** , a request that runs on a schedule instead of when you ask.
See also *recurring task*.

## C

**Connector** , a link to an account that belongs to you, such as Google, Slack
or Notion. The connector decides whether GENI is allowed somewhere; what it can
do there is decided by the *skill*. See [Connectors](../connectors/README.md).

**Credit** , the unit of account for work. Every action uses credits from your
monthly balance, and you see the amount on the button before you start.
See [The credit system](../hoe-het-werkt/credits.md).

**Credit pack** , a one-off top-up of credits, separate from your subscription.
Available from Starter; on Free there is a hard limit.

## G

**GENI** , the name of your AI assistant. One assistant you meet in the
dashboard, in Telegram and through voice, with the same memory and the same
skills everywhere. See [What is dGENIX](../concepten/wat-is-dgenix.md).

**GEO** , Generative Engine Optimization: becoming findable and quotable in
answers from AI search engines such as ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI
Overviews. It is the counterpart to SEO for a world where people read answers
instead of links.

**GEO Engine** , the engine that measures whether AI search engines can read your
site and whether they mention you, and delivers concrete improvements. Available
from Pro.

**Growth Tools** , the in-app name for the five engines together: SEO, GEO,
Authority, Reputation and AI Content. On the website they are called Growth
Engines.

## K

**Knowledge base** , the place where you upload files GENI uses as background
knowledge, such as a product catalogue or your terms and conditions. Available
from Growth. See [Knowledge base](../functies/kennisbank.md).

**Knowledge document** , the text you create during the intake, which GENI reads
in every conversation: who you are, what you do and how it should write.

## M

**MCP** , Model Context Protocol: an open standard for connecting external tools
to GENI that are not in the marketplace by default. See
[MCP connectors](../connectors/mcp-connectors.md).

**Memory** , the individual facts, preferences and goals GENI remembers about you
and reuses later. Eight types, strictly per user.
See [Memory](../functies/geheugen.md).

## P

**Page Analysis** , a deep analysis of one page: score, keyword positions, Core
Web Vitals and concrete recommendations. Not to be confused with the *Site
Crawl*, which goes broad instead.

**Project** , a walled-off workspace with its own instructions, conversations and
memories. Meant for working separately per client or brand. Available from
Growth. See [Projects](../functies/projecten.md).

## R

**Recurring task** , a request that runs automatically on a schedule (daily,
weekly, monthly). How many you get depends on your plan: Starter 1, Growth 5,
Pro 10, Business 20. See [Scheduled tasks](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md).

**Reputation Engine** , the engine that collects reviews in a GDPR-safe way
through email campaigns with mandatory opt-out. Available from Pro.

## S

**SEO Engine** , the engine that analyses your site for technical and editorial
findability in classic search engines, with a score and a ranked list of
improvements. Available from Growth.

**Site Crawl** , a broad scan across your whole site, up to a hundred pages, with
mechanical checks such as duplicate titles and broken links. The counterpart to
the *Page Analysis*, which goes deep on one page.

**Skill** , a capability you switch on that lets GENI actually *do* something,
from reading mail to auditing a website. A skill without a *connector* cannot
reach anything. See [Skills explained](../handleiding/skills-uitgelegd.md).

## T

**Telegram** , a free extra channel alongside the dashboard. Everything stays in
sync: same assistant, same memory. You never need it.
See [Connect Telegram](../aan-de-slag/telegram-koppelen.md).

## W

**Workspace** , the place in the dashboard for your notes, tasks and timeline.
The core is free on every plan; the smart features run on credits from Starter.
See [Workspace](../functies/werkruimte.md).
