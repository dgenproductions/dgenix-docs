# Connect Google Calendar

By connecting Google Calendar, your AI assistant can schedule appointments, check your calendar and remind you of meetings.

> Google Calendar is included in **all plans**, Starter, Growth and Pro.

---

## What can your assistant do with Google Calendar?

- Schedule appointments and send invites
- Get your calendar for today or this week
- Check whether you are available for a meeting
- Remind you of upcoming appointments

---

## Connecting Google Calendar

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** next to Google Calendar
3. A window opens, log in with the Google account you want to connect
4. Grant dGENIX the requested permissions
5. The window closes automatically, the integration is active right away

---

## Example commands

- `"Schedule a meeting with Jan Friday at 14:00 and send him an invite"`
- `"What's on my calendar today?"`
- `"Am I available Tuesday at 10:00?"`
- `"Move my Monday meeting to Wednesday"`

---

## Which permissions does dGENIX request?

| Permission | For |
|---|---|
| Read calendar | Fetch appointments and check availability |
| Edit calendar | Create, change and delete appointments |

dGENIX only changes your calendar on your command.

---

## Disconnecting Google Calendar

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Remove"** next to Google Calendar
3. The connection is removed immediately

You can also revoke access via [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## Problems?

**Connection failed:** make sure you are logged in to the right Google account and try again.
