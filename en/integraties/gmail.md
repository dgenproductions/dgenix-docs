# Connect Gmail

By connecting Gmail, your AI assistant can read your inbox, categorise emails and write draft replies.

> Gmail is included in **all plans**, Starter, Growth and Pro.

---

## What can your assistant do with Gmail?

- Fetch and summarise unread emails
- Categorise emails (action needed, informational, promotional)
- Write and send draft replies after your confirmation
- Notify you when urgent messages arrive

Your assistant **only sends emails after your explicit confirmation**.

---

## Connecting Gmail

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** next to Gmail
3. A window opens, log in with the Google account you want to connect
4. Grant dGENIX the requested permissions
5. The window closes automatically, the integration is active right away

---

## Which permissions does dGENIX request?

| Permission | For |
|---|---|
| Read emails | Fetch and summarise your inbox |
| Send emails | Send emails after your confirmation |

dGENIX **cannot** delete emails or manage your account.

---

## Disconnecting Gmail

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Remove"** next to Gmail
3. The connection is removed immediately

You can also revoke access via [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## Problems?

**Connection failed:** try again and make sure you are logged in to the right Google account in your browser.
