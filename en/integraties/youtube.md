# Connect YouTube

By connecting your YouTube channel, your AI assistant can fetch live channel statistics, generate video ideas, create upload checklists and show analytics insights.

> YouTube Manager is available from the **Growth plan**.

---

## What can your assistant do with YouTube?

- Fetch channel statistics and recent videos
- Generate video ideas based on your best content
- Create SEO-optimised upload checklists
- Get per-video statistics (views, likes, comments)
- Analyse comments for content opportunities and questions
- Analytics report on watch time, CTR and channel growth
- Determine the best upload day and time based on your audience

Your assistant only reads your channel data, **no videos are uploaded or changed**.

---

## Connecting step by step

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** next to YouTube Manager
3. Log in with the Google account linked to your YouTube channel
4. Grant dGENIX the requested permissions (YouTube + Analytics)
5. The window closes automatically, the integration is active right away

> Connected YouTube before? Disconnect and reconnect to access Analytics features (report, audience insights).

---

## Which permissions does dGENIX request?

| Permission | For |
| --- | --- |
| Read YouTube channel data | Fetch statistics, videos and channel info |
| Read YouTube Analytics | Watch-time report, CTR, traffic sources, upload times |

dGENIX **cannot** upload, edit or delete videos.

---

## Disconnecting YouTube

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Remove"** next to YouTube Manager
3. The connection is removed immediately

You can also revoke access via [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## Problems?

**Connection failed:** check that you are logged in to the Google account linked to your YouTube channel.

**Analytics unavailable:** disconnect and reconnect, your current connection may not have Analytics permissions.
