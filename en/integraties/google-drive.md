# Connect Google Drive

By connecting Google Drive, your AI assistant can fetch files, summarise documents and save results.

> Google Drive is included in **all plans**, Starter, Growth and Pro.

---

## What can your assistant do with Google Drive?

- Fetch and summarise documents and spreadsheets
- Find files by name or content
- Save results as a Google Doc or spreadsheet
- Collaborate on documents by analysing them in chat

---

## Connecting Google Drive

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** next to Google Drive
3. A window opens, log in with the Google account you want to connect
4. Grant dGENIX the requested permissions
5. The window closes automatically, the integration is active right away

---

## Example commands

- `"Summarise the document 'Project plan Q2'"`
- `"Find the latest quote file I saved"`
- `"Save the summary of this conversation as a Google Doc"`

---

## Which permissions does dGENIX request?

| Permission | For |
|---|---|
| Read files | Fetch and summarise documents |
| Create/edit files | Save results as a new document |

dGENIX only has access to files you explicitly specify or that your assistant creates.

---

## Disconnecting Google Drive

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Remove"** next to Google Drive
3. The connection is removed immediately

You can also revoke access via [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

---

## Problems?

**Connection failed:** make sure you are logged in to the right Google account and try again.
