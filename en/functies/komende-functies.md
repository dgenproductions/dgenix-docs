# Upcoming features

This page lists what is coming soon and what shipped recently, so you know what you can and cannot count on.

New skills and connections appear in your marketplace automatically once they
are ready. There is nothing to install or update.

## Coming soon

| What | Plan | What it is waiting on |
|---|---|---|
| [Microsoft 365](../connectors/microsoft-365.md) | Growth+ | Azure OAuth verification at Microsoft |
| [Canva](../connectors/canva.md) | Starter+ | Approval from the Canva Developer programme |
| [MCP connectors](../connectors/mcp-connectors.md) | Growth+ | Expanding step by step |

**Microsoft 365** brings Outlook mail, your calendar and reading along in Teams
to GENI, so you do not have to move to Google to use it.

**Canva** lets GENI search designs, fill brand templates and export, without you
opening the editor.

**MCP connectors** is not a single connection but an open standard that lets
tools be plugged in faster than building a connection for each one by hand.

## What you see

While something is not yet available, it simply sits in your marketplace with a
**Coming soon** label. You cannot activate it and nothing is charged. Once it
goes live the label disappears and you get a notification.

What is available you recognise by the activate button. Anything without a label
works today.

## Limits

- **No dates are given.** Microsoft and Canva decide when they approve; naming a date we cannot keep helps nobody.
- **This list is not exhaustive.** Smaller improvements do not make this page.
- **A requested skill is not a promise.** We weigh every request, but not everything gets built.
- **Coming soon items cannot be activated early**, not even on a higher plan.

## Troubleshooting

**A skill shows as Coming soon while you need it.** Check whether an existing skill solves the same thing. For Microsoft 365 that is often the Google suite; for Canva, [AI image generation](../skills/ai-beeldgeneratie.md).

**You do not see a new skill.** Refresh your dashboard. New skills appear automatically, but an open tab does not fetch by itself.

**You are missing a connection you saw elsewhere.** Some connections sit in a higher plan. The skill card states which plan it starts from.

## Requesting something

Need a skill or connection that is not there? Use the **Request skill** button in
your dashboard or the [contact page](https://dgenix.com/contact?type=skill). What gets asked for
often moves up the list.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a new skill cost extra?**
Almost never. Skills are included in your plan; you pay credits per run. See
[The credit system](../hoe-het-werkt/credits.md).

**Will I be told when something goes live?**
Yes, you get a notification in the dashboard.

**Can I join a test?**
Sometimes we test with a small group. Let us know through the contact page if
you want to take part.
