# Create an account

dGENIX is free to start.

---

## Step by step

1. Go to [app.dgenix.com/signup](https://app.dgenix.com/signup)
2. Enter:
   - Your email address
   - A password (at least 8 characters)
3. Click **"Create account"**
4. You receive a confirmation email, click the link inside

After confirming, you reach the plan picker.

---

## Choosing a plan

Right after signing up you pick a plan:

- **Free**, explore for free, no credit card needed
- **Starter / Growth / Pro / Business**, paid via Stripe, with more credits and skills
- **Custom**, on request, for teams and white label

You can switch any time via **Dashboard -> Account -> Subscription**.

[See all plans](../plannen-en-prijzen/README.md)

---

## Next: intake and tour

After choosing a plan you activate your assistant and go through a short **intake** (give GENI a name and a working style) and a **tour** of the platform. Then you are ready to give commands.

---

## Already have an account?

Log in at [app.dgenix.com/login](https://app.dgenix.com/login).

## Forgot your password?

The login page has a "Forgot password?" link. Enter your email and you receive a reset link.
