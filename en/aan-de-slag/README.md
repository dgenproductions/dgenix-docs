# Getting started

dGENIX is live in about 5 minutes. You work from the dashboard at [app.dgenix.com](https://app.dgenix.com). No server, no technical knowledge, no installation.

---

## What do you need?

- An email address
- That's it. You start for free, no credit card required.

---

## The steps

### 1. Create an account

Go to [app.dgenix.com/signup](https://app.dgenix.com/signup) and create an account with your email and a password.

[Create an account](account-aanmaken.md)

### 2. Choose a plan

After signing up you pick a plan. Start free with **Free**, or go straight to Starter, Growth, Pro or Business. You can always switch later.

[See the plans](../plannen-en-prijzen/README.md)

### 3. Set up your assistant (intake)

In a short intake you give GENI a name, a role and a working style (tone, expertise, your field). That way your assistant knows exactly how to help you.

### 4. Connect your tools

Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** and connect your own accounts: Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets and more. One click, through a secure connection.

[See the connectors](../connectors/README.md)

### 5. Activate skills

Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate what you need. Stack skills into a process that runs itself.

[Skills marketplace](../skills/README.md)

---

## Done, give your first command

You work straight from the dashboard. A few examples:

> "Give me an overview of my unread emails"

> "Plan tomorrow: meeting at 9am, presentation at 2pm"

> "Find 10 leads in the local hospitality scene and write a first email"

GENI runs the task and delivers the result back.

---

## Take it on the go (optional)

Want to reach GENI via Telegram too? Telegram is a **free, optional add-on** that you activate whenever you like via **Dashboard -> Skills**. It is not required to start.

[Connect Telegram](telegram-koppelen.md)
