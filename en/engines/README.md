# Growth Tools, the 5 Growth Engines

Besides the individual skills, dGENIX has five **[Growth Engines](https://dgenix.com/engines)**: the heaviest, outcome-driven tools on the platform. They measure, write and publish. GENI runs them via chat and you schedule them via Automations. You find them in the dashboard under **Growth Tools** (`/dashboard/engines`).

Fixed order: SEO, GEO, Authority, Reputation, AI Content.

At the top of Growth Tools is the **Overview** tab: one combined dashboard with your latest SEO, GEO and Authority scores per site (plus AI Share-of-Voice, keywords in the top 100 and traffic value), with a progress chart per engine, the comparison versus your competitor and a prioritized **"what to fix next" list** that surfaces the highest-impact actions from your audits and positions.

**For agencies:** the **portfolio overview** shows all your client sites in one table with the latest SEO, GEO and Authority scores plus traffic value per site (click a row to jump to that client). Deliver **white-label reports** (from Pro): put your own agency name, logo and accent colour on the PDF audit reports. And in the AI assistant, create a **project** (chat folder) per client and use "To project" to add that client's audits and fixes. GENI then knows that context in every chat inside the project, so you can work, plan and report per client.

> **Want to try it free?** At [Free scan](https://dgenix.com/demo) you can scan your website for free on SEO and AI visibility (GEO), no account needed. You get your score and the top issues instantly. The full results and the engines themselves sit behind a (free) account.

## 1. SEO Engine

**Rank higher on Google.** A real site crawl (up to 100 pages, 100+ checks), keyword research with search volume and intent, a **Rank Tracker** that follows your keyword positions over time daily or weekly (with AI-Overview visibility and the competitor per keyword) and alerts you the moment a keyword drops or falls out of the top 10, a **SEO fix generator** that writes a ready-to-paste SEO title + meta description per page to drop into any CMS, and a Links & Authority pillar (Authority Score, backlinks, the link gap versus your competitor). You get concrete actions and a client-ready report.

You apply fixes your way: hand the PDF audit report to your web builder or developer, use the fix generator (ready-to-paste title/meta for any CMS, per page or for a whole list of URLs in bulk with CSV export), or ask GENI for an explanation and guidance in the chat. One-click auto-apply to WordPress and Shopify is coming soon. You can also have the audits **re-run automatically** (weekly/monthly) so your Overview, Missions, trends and alerts refresh by themselves.

- Plan: Growth+
- [Read more](../skills/seo-engine.md)

## 2. GEO Engine

**Get cited by AI.** GENI measures how often your brand shows up in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude, your AI Share-of-Voice, plus AI search volume, AI Overview tracking, the gap versus your competitor and your Local SEO. Includes crawler access, a citability score and llms.txt.

- Plan: Pro+
- [Read more](../skills/geo-engine.md)

## 3. Authority Engine

**From measuring to publishing.** GENI writes the content too: a topical map with pillar and cluster articles, a content score against the live SERP, and draft-first publishing to 8 CMS platforms. Nothing goes live without your approval.

- Plan: Pro+
- [Read more](../skills/authority-engine.md)

## 4. Reputation Engine

**More and better reviews.** GENI automatically asks your customers for a review via a branded email with a direct Google review link. Import contacts from Google Sheets or CSV, with a mandatory opt-out link (GDPR), suppression list and a monthly cap.

- Plan: Pro+
- [Read more](../skills/reputation-engine.md)

## 5. AI Content Engine

**One video into a week of content.** Give GENI one long video; you get the best moments back as ready-to-post vertical clips, each with a virality score and a content pack (titles, caption, hashtags).

- Plan: Growth+
- [Read more](../skills/ai-content-engine.md)

Ask GENI in one sentence, or let it run automatically every week via [Automations](../functies/workflow-automations.md).
