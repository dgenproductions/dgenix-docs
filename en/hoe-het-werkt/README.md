# What your AI assistant does

GENI, your AI assistant, is not a chatbot. It runs real tasks and delivers results, from the dashboard at [app.dgenix.com](https://app.dgenix.com).

---

## A few examples

### Gmail

Connect your Gmail and GENI manages your inbox: it reads messages, categorises them and drafts replies for emails that need one.

> "Give me an overview of my unread emails"
> "Draft a reply to Jan's latest email"

### Google Calendar

Connect Google Calendar and GENI manages your agenda: scheduling meetings, giving you a daily overview and helping you plan appointments.

> "Schedule a meeting with Jan on Friday at 2pm"
> "What's on my calendar tomorrow?"

### Google Drive

Connect Google Drive and GENI finds, opens and creates files for you.

> "Find the document about the Q1 report"
> "Create a new document: meeting notes 3 April"

### Social Media Manager

Write posts for LinkedIn, Instagram, X and Facebook based on your input.

> "Write a LinkedIn post about our new AI automation service"

[See all skills](../skills/README.md)

## How does execution work?

Every command you give is handled by your personal AI assistant, which:

1. Receives your message (via the dashboard, or Telegram if you have the add-on)
2. Decides which action and skills are needed
3. Runs the steps (fetch, process, write, schedule)
4. Saves results where relevant (files, calendar, sheets)
5. Sends the result back in the dashboard

---

## Scheduled tasks

Via **Dashboard -> Tasks** you set up recurring commands. GENI runs them automatically on the schedule you set (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly), with no effort from you.

---

## What does a task cost?

Every task uses credits, depending on its complexity:

| Task type | ~Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Read / summarise email | 5-15 cr |
| Schedule an appointment | ~10 cr |
| Find a file in Drive | ~5 cr |
| Write a social post | ~2 cr |
| AI chat message | ~20 cr |

[More about credits](credits.md)

---

## What does GENI NOT do?

- It does not send emails on your behalf without confirmation (drafts are prepared)
- It does not take or make payments
- It does not delete files or emails unless you explicitly ask

You stay in control at all times.
