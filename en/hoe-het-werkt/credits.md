# The credit system

dGENIX runs on credits. Every task GENI, your AI assistant, performs uses credits. So you only pay for what you actually use.

---

## What are credits?

Credits are the unit of account in dGENIX. They are used when GENI:

- Uses an AI language model to think, write or summarise
- Fetches and processes data (leads, emails, research results)
- Runs a skill (for example reading Gmail, generating an image, running an SEO audit)

An average task costs about 100 credits. A short chat costs less, a heavy action (a full site audit, an AI video with clips) more. You pay in proportion to the work.

---

## How many credits per month?

| Plan | Credits/month | ~Tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Free | 1,000 (hard cap) |, |
| Starter | 75,000 | ~750 |
| Growth | 175,000 | ~1,750 |
| Pro | 350,000 | ~3,500 |
| Business | 1,000,000 | ~10,000 |
| Custom | Tailored | Unlimited |

Your credits are topped up automatically each month on your billing date. **Unused credits expire** at the end of the period, they do not carry over.

## What does a task cost? (guide prices)

| Task | ~Credits |
|---|---|
| AI chat message (short) | ~20 cr |
| Read email / summarise inbox | ~5-15 cr |
| Schedule an appointment | ~10 cr |
| Find a file in Drive | ~5 cr |
| Social media post | ~2 cr |
| Web search | 75 cr per query |
| Generate an AI image (Nano Banana) | 500-1,700 cr |
| Transcribe audio | 12-20 cr per minute |
| SEO Engine audit | 1,500-22,000 cr (by depth) |
| GEO Engine audit | 8,000-95,000 cr (by depth) |
| AI Content Engine (video -> clips) | 500 cr + 600 cr per clip |

> A skill's exact cost is always shown on the button in the dashboard before you start. If a paid action fails, the credits are refunded automatically.

---

## What if I run out of credits?

If your balance gets too low, GENI stops running new paid tasks and you get a notification. You can then:

- **Buy more** (from Starter), a one-time [credit pack](../plannen-en-prijzen/credits-bijkopen.md) (S to XXL)
- **Wait for renewal**, credits top up on your billing date
- **Upgrade**, to a plan with more credits per month

As soon as your balance is sufficient again, GENI continues automatically.

---

## Where do I see my credit balance?

- **Dashboard -> Home**, current balance, percentage used and your renewal date, at the top of the stat row.
- **Dashboard -> Account -> Usage**, a detailed overview of every transaction: which actions used how many credits, per day and per feature.

---

## Engine credits (Growth Engines subscription)

Using the SEO, GEO, Authority or backlink tools heavily? You can take an optional **Growth Engines subscription** with a separate **engine-credit wallet**. That wallet is topped up every month and is used **first** for engine actions, only then your regular plan credits. That way you don't spend your regular credits on heavy audits. Engine credits work only for the engines. See [Plans & Pricing](../plannen-en-prijzen/README.md) for the tiers. The Growth Tools hub shows how many engine credits you have left this month.
