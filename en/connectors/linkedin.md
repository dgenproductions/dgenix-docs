# Connect LinkedIn

Connect your LinkedIn profile to publish posts and fetch statistics via your AI assistant.

---

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** on LinkedIn
3. Log in with your LinkedIn account
4. Grant the requested permissions
5. After approval LinkedIn shows as **Connected**

---

## Permissions

| Scope | For |
|---|---|
| `r_liteprofile` | Read profile data |
| `r_emailaddress` | Get the email address |
| `w_member_social` | Publish posts |

> **Note:** LinkedIn requires approval of the `w_member_social` scope via their Developer Program. This can take 1-2 weeks. Reading the profile works right after connecting.

---

## Removing the connection

Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** -> click LinkedIn -> **"Disconnect"**.
