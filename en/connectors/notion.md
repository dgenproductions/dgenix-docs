# Connect Notion

Connect your Notion workspace to create, update and search pages via your AI assistant.

---

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** on Notion
3. Log in with your Notion account
4. Select the pages or databases you want to grant access to
5. Click **"Allow access"**
6. After approval Notion shows as **Connected**

---

## Note: access scope

Notion works with a page-selection model. Your assistant only has access to the pages and databases you explicitly selected when connecting. Want to add more pages? Disconnect and repeat the authorisation.

---

## Removing the connection

Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** -> click Notion -> **"Disconnect"**.
