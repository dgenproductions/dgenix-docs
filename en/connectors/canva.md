# Connect Canva

> **Coming soon**, the Canva connection is waiting on approval from Canva's Developer API programme.

---

## What's supported

After activation:

- Search and reuse existing designs
- Create blank designs
- Auto-fill brand templates
- Export designs as PNG, PDF, MP4 or GIF

---

## Steps (once available)

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** on Canva
3. Log in with your Canva account and grant access
4. The Canva skill is active right away
