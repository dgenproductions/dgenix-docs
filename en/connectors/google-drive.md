# Connect Google Drive

See [Connect Gmail](gmail.md), Google Drive is part of the same Google Workspace connection. After authorising your Google account, Gmail, Calendar, Drive and Sheets are all available.

---

## Permissions for Drive

| Scope | For |
|---|---|
| `drive` | Find, read, create, move and share files |

The full scope gives your assistant access to all files in your Drive, needed for searching, summarising, creating folders, moving files and setting up shares.
