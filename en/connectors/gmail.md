# Connect Gmail

Connect your Google account once to give your assistant access to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Sheets.

---

## Steps

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **"Connect"** on Gmail (or Google Workspace)
3. You are redirected to the Google consent screen
4. Log in with your Google account and grant the requested permissions
5. After approval the connector shows as **Connected**

---

## Which skills does this unlock?

After connecting Google, these skills are available right away:

- **Gmail**, read, write and send emails
- **Google Calendar**, view your calendar and schedule appointments
- **Google Drive**, find, manage and share files
- **Google Sheets**, read and edit spreadsheets

---

## Permissions

dGENIX requests the following Google scopes:

| Scope | For |
|---|---|
| `gmail.modify` | Read, send and manage labels |
| `calendar` | Read the calendar and create appointments |
| `drive` | Read, create, move and share files in Drive |
| `spreadsheets` | Read and edit Sheets |

---

## Removing the connection

Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** -> click the Google connector -> **"Disconnect"**.
