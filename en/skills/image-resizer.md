# AI Image Editing

## What does this skill do?

Send an image and give a command, the assistant adjusts the format, background or style. Handy for social media formats, product photos and marketing images.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** no extra connections needed

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **AI Image Editing** and click Activate
3. Send an image as an attachment in the chat

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Adjust this image to a square format for Instagram"
- "Make this product photo 1080×1080 pixels"
- "Convert to 9:16 for an Instagram Reel thumbnail"
- "Shrink this image to max 800 pixels wide"

**Supported formats:**

- JPEG, PNG, WebP
- Output formats: 1:1, 9:16, 16:9, 4:3

**What the assistant does:**

1. Processes the attached image
2. Applies the desired format or dimensions
3. Saves the result in your **Files -> Images** in the dashboard
