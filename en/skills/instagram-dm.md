# Instagram DM Manager

Manage your Instagram Direct Messages via your AI assistant. Read incoming DMs, qualify leads and draft replies, straight from chat or the dashboard.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Read and categorise incoming Instagram DMs
- Draft replies to messages
- Recognise leads and pass them on for follow-up
- Handle FAQs automatically
- Summarise conversations for an overview

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** Instagram Business account linked to the Meta Developer API

---

## How do you activate Instagram DM Manager?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Instagram DM Manager
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Enter your Instagram Business API credentials (Access Token, Business Account ID)
5. The skill is active right away

---
