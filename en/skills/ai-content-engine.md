# AI Content Engine

The [AI Content Engine](https://dgenix.com/engines/ai-content) automatically cuts a long video into up to fifteen short clips, scores how promising each one is and supplies titles, a caption and hashtags with it.

The manual work this replaces is rewatching an hour of video to find the usable
moments. Available from **Pro**.

## What the AI Content Engine does

| Part | What it produces |
|---|---|
| **Clips** | Up to 15 short fragments, cut on meaningful moments |
| **Virality score** | A 0 to 100 grade per clip, so you know which to post first |
| **Content pack** | Three titles, a caption, 15 to 20 hashtags and an improved opening line |
| **Burned-in subtitles** | Optional, for platforms where people watch without sound |

## Example: what you put in, what you get back

You paste a YouTube link to a 42-minute interview and ask for 5 clips. A few
minutes later:

> **Clip 3 , 0:47** · score **87** (High)
>
> *"The biggest mistake I made was hiring too early"*
>
> **Titles:** "The mistake that cost me 40,000" · "Do NOT hire too early" · "What nobody tells you about scaling"
>
> **Caption:** Three years ago I thought growing meant more people. It did not.
>
> **Hashtags:** #entrepreneurship #smallbusiness #scaling #leadership #startup (+13)
>
> [Download] [Copy pack]

The score is the point: you do not get five equivalent clips but a ranking, so
you know which one goes out on Monday and which one can wait.

## How to use it

1. Open **AI Content Engine** in the sidebar
2. Paste a YouTube URL or a direct video URL
3. Choose the number of clips, 1 to 15, default 5
4. Optionally turn on **Burn in captions**
5. Click **Generate viral clips**
6. Wait 5 to 10 minutes; processing runs in the background, you can close the screen
7. The clips appear with scores, content packs and download buttons

## How the virality score works

The score is a weighted judgement across five dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight | Why it counts |
|---|---|---|
| Hook strength, the opening line | 30% | The first two seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching |
| Emotional intensity | 20% | Flat fragments rarely get shared |
| Engagement potential | 20% | Does it invite replies or forwarding |
| Curiosity gap | 15% | Does it promise something you have to stay for |
| Speech variation and pace | 15% | Monotone delivery loses viewers |

**Labels:** High (80-100), Medium (50-79), Low (under 50).

A low score does not mean a clip is bad, only that it will probably do less well
as a standalone fragment.

## Limits

- **Up to 15 clips** per video.
- **The video has to be publicly reachable.** A private or unlisted video, or one behind a login, cannot be fetched.
- **Spoken content works best.** The analysis leans on the transcript, so music videos and footage without speech yield little.
- **No automatic publishing.** You download the clips and post them yourself; the engine does not post to social channels.
- **The crop is optimised for vertical** short-form video; widescreen footage gets cropped.
- **Burning in subtitles cannot be undone**; generate again if you decide against them.
- **Processing takes minutes, not seconds.** For a long video expect the upper end of 5 to 10 minutes.

## Troubleshooting

**The video cannot be fetched.** It is private, deleted or region-restricted. Test the URL in an incognito window; if you cannot see it there, neither can we.

**Processing seems stuck.** Feel free to close the screen; it runs in the background and clips appear by themselves. If it takes longer than fifteen minutes, start again; failed runs are refunded.

**The clips are cut at the wrong moments.** The engine cuts on topic transitions in the transcript. Heavy background noise or people talking over each other makes that harder.

**All the scores are low.** Usually with a video without clear statements or stories. Material with concrete anecdotes and opinions scores higher.

**The hashtags do not fit my market.** They follow the clip's content, not your industry. Adjust them before posting, or ask GENI in chat for a set that suits your audience.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which video sources are supported?**
YouTube URLs and direct links to a video file.

**Can GENI schedule this?**
You can start processing and ask for its status through chat. Judging and
posting clips stays human work, because that is exactly where your taste is
needed.

**What language are the titles and captions in?**
They follow the language of the video.

**What is the difference with the Short Generator?**
The Short Generator makes shorts out of a video. The AI Content Engine adds
scoring, three title variants, a caption, hashtags and a rewritten hook, so you
get a ranking and ready-to-use copy.

**May I make clips from someone else's video?**
Technically you can, legally it is your responsibility. Use material you hold
the rights to.
