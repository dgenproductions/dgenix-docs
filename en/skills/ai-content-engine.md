# AI Content Engine

## What does this skill do?

Paste a YouTube URL or direct video link and the AI Content Engine automatically generates up to 15 viral short-form clips. Each clip gets a virality score (0-100), three viral title suggestions, a caption and 15-20 hashtags. Captions can optionally be burned into the video. Everything is available in the dashboard once processing is done.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro+
- **Integrations:** no extra connections needed

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **AI Content Engine** and click **Activate** (free on Pro+)

After activation, **AI Content Engine** appears in the sidebar.

## How do you use it?

1. Go to **AI Content Engine** via the sidebar
2. Paste a YouTube URL or direct video URL
3. Choose the number of clips (1-15, default 5)
4. Optional: turn on **Burn in captions**
5. Click **Generate viral clips**
6. Wait 5-10 minutes, processing runs in the background
7. Clips appear automatically with scores, content packs and download buttons

## Virality score

Each clip gets a hybrid score (0-100) based on five dimensions:

| Dimension | Weight |
| --- | --- |
| Hook strength (opening line) | 30% |
| Emotional intensity | 20% |
| Engagement potential | 20% |
| Curiosity gap | 15% |
| Speech variation / pace | 15% |

**Labels:** High (80-100), Medium (50-79), Low (under 50)

## Content packs

For each clip the AI automatically generates:

- **3 viral titles**, optimised for clicks
- **Caption**, platform-neutral, ready to copy
- **15-20 hashtags**, relevant to the clip's content
- **Hook rewrite**, improved opening line for maximum retention
