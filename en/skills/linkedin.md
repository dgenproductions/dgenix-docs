# LinkedIn Automation

Publish LinkedIn posts and view statistics via your AI assistant. Write, optimise and post content to your profile, without opening LinkedIn yourself.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Write LinkedIn posts and publish them directly
- Get post statistics (views, likes, comments)
- Look up profile data for context
- Plan and run content on request

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** LinkedIn OAuth connection (one-time setup)

---

## How do you activate LinkedIn Automation?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on LinkedIn Automation
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on LinkedIn
5. Log in with your LinkedIn account and grant access
6. The skill is active after connecting

> **Note:** publishing posts via the API requires LinkedIn approval for the `w_member_social` scope. This can take 1-2 weeks after creating the developer app. Connecting your account works immediately.
