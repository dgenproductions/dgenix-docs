# GEO Engine

## What does this skill do?

The GEO Engine analyses your website for visibility in AI search engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Google AI Overviews. You get a detailed audit with scores per category, a list of issues and concrete recommendations to improve your AI visibility.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro+ (included)
- **Integrations:** none, the audit fetches your public website directly

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate the **GEO Engine** (or open it directly if you are on Pro+)
2. Go via the side menu to **Growth Tools -> GEO Engine**
3. Enter your website URL and click **Add site**
4. Choose an audit depth and start

## What can you do with it?

### What is measured?

The **GEO Score** (0-100) is made up of five factors:

| Factor | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Citability | 30% | How well your content can be cited by AI (direct answers, length, readability, concrete figures) |
| Crawler Access | 20% | Whether the 14 AI bots can reach your site (do not block retrieval bots) |
| Entity & Authority | 20% | Organization schema, sameAs links, Person schema, does AI recognise your brand as an entity |
| Technical | 15% | HTTPS, readable HTML content, canonical, real Core Web Vitals |
| Schema | 15% | Completeness of JSON-LD schema markup |

### AI Share of Voice (Max Audit)

The **Max Audit** is the real differentiator. We generate realistic customer questions in your niche and put them to **Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity**. We then measure how often your brand is mentioned or cited, your **Share of Voice** in AI answers, per engine and as a total percentage. Plus JS rendering and real Core Web Vitals.

The Max Audit also shows:

- **Competitor comparison**, add a competitor and see your Share of Voice side by side.
- **Cited sources**, which domains the AI engines mention (your site and the competitor's marked).
- **Per-prompt visibility**, in which questions your brand is or is not mentioned, per engine.
- **Sentiment**, the tone in which your brand is mentioned in AI answers (positive, neutral or negative) with a short note.

Where classic SEO measures positions in Google, this measures whether AI actually names you, increasingly the first place customers get an answer.

### AI visibility meter, trend & AI search volume

On top of Share-of-Voice the GEO Engine shows:

- **AI visibility meter**, one clear status ("Great / Good") based on your Share-of-Voice.
- **Trend per engine**, your AI visibility over time, total and per engine (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity).
- **AI search volume**, how often your core keywords are asked inside AI tools (ChatGPT etc.), the demand side next to your visibility.
- **Competitor gap**, the prompts where your competitor is mentioned and you are not, plus the sources that cite them and not you.

### AI Overviews tracking

Besides AI chatbots, Google now has **AI Overviews** at the top of search results. In the GEO Engine you can check per keyword whether an AI Overview appears and whether your site is **cited** in it (with your organic position). Repeated checks build a trend. **400 credits per keyword.**

### Local SEO, Google Business Profile

The GEO Engine includes a **Local SEO card**: type a business name + location and you get the **rating**, the **number of reviews**, the **category** and the **GBP status** (claimed/not claimed). Works for any business, including competitors. **1,500 credits** per check.

## What is llms.txt?

`llms.txt` is a standard file (similar to `robots.txt`) that tells AI search engines which content they may read and index. The GEO Engine generates a tailored `llms.txt` file based on your website content.

More info: [llmstxt.org](https://llmstxt.org)
