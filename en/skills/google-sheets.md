# Google Sheets

With the Google Sheets skill your assistant reads, writes and analyses data in your spreadsheets.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Pull data from a sheet and summarise it
- Add rows and columns based on your input
- Analyse existing data and give insights
- Explain or adjust formulas
- Search sheets for specific values

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter, Growth or Pro (included in all plans)
- **Integration:** connect a Google account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

---

## How do you activate Google Sheets?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Google Sheets
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on Google Sheets and complete the Google consent screen
5. After approval Sheets is active right away

---

## Example commands

```
Give me the total revenue from my sales sheet for April
```
```
Add a row to "Clients.xlsx": name=Jansen, company=XYZ Ltd, date=17-04-2026
```
```
What is the average order value in column D?
```
