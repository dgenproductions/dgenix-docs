# Google Drive

With the Google Drive skill your assistant finds, reads and uploads files in your Drive.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Find files by name, type or content
- Summarise documents and extract key points
- Create new folders in your Drive structure
- Move files to another folder
- Rename files via a chat message
- Share files with colleagues or make them public
- Set view, comment or edit rights per person
- Upload files from the dashboard (via Assets)

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter, Growth or Pro (included in all plans)
- **Integration:** connect a Google account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

---

## How do you activate Google Drive?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Google Drive
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on Google Drive and complete the Google consent screen
5. After approval Drive is active right away

---

## Example commands

```
Find the document "Quote ABC" in my Drive
```
```
Give me a summary of the report in "Monthly reports/April"
```
```
Create a new folder: "Clients/2026/Q2"
```
