# AI Receptionist

## What does this skill do?

The AI Receptionist answers FAQs automatically based on a FAQ database you configure. Set it up once with business info and common questions, and it handles questions from customers, staff or visitors and summarises conversations.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro or higher
- **Integrations:** none

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in the dashboard
2. Find **AI Receptionist** and click **Activate**
3. Configure the FAQ via the assistant (see below)

## Configuring

**Set up the FAQ:**
> "Configure the AI Receptionist for 'De Stijl Salon'. FAQ: opening hours Mon-Fri 9-6, Saturday 9-5. Appointments via 040-1234567. Prices: haircut €25, colour from €45."

**Answer FAQs:**
> "Answer the question: 'Are you open on Sundays?'"

**Summarise a conversation:**
> "Summarise this conversation and note action items"

## Credit cost

| Action | Credits |
|---|---|
| Configure FAQ | 5 cr |
| Answer a question | 3 cr |
| Summarise a conversation | 5 cr |
