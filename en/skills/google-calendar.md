# Google Calendar

With the Google Calendar skill your assistant views, schedules and manages your calendar via chat.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Get your daily schedule from a chat message
- Schedule appointments with date, time and location
- Detect conflicts and suggest alternatives
- Create and send meeting invites
- Manage recurring appointments

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter, Growth or Pro (included in all plans)
- **Integration:** connect a Google account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

---

## How do you activate Google Calendar?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Google Calendar
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on Google Calendar and complete the Google consent screen
5. After approval Calendar is active right away

---

## Example commands

```
What's on my calendar tomorrow?
```
```
Schedule an appointment on Friday 25 April at 14:00, "Client call ABC"
```
```
Do I have free time on Tuesday or Thursday this week?
```
