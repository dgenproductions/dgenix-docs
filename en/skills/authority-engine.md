# Authority Engine

The **Authority Engine** is the step from *measuring* to *doing*: GENI writes improved content and places it in your CMS, but **never without your approval**. Safe for your search presence.

## How it works

1. **Connect your CMS**, link WordPress (Application Password) or Webflow (API key + collection ID) in the Authority dashboard.
2. **Generate content**, give a topic (and optionally a target keyword). GENI writes an original, E-E-A-T-focused article. This stays a **draft inside dGENIX**, nothing goes to your site.
   - **Or build a topical map**, give a main topic and GENI creates a **pillar + cluster articles** (keyword clustering). Generate a draft per item; cluster articles link internally to the pillar automatically, the proven structure for topical authority.
3. **Review in the queue**, inspect the draft in the approval queue.
4. **You decide**, per item:
   - **To CMS as draft**, placed as a draft in your CMS (you publish there yourself).
   - **Publish live**, put live on your site directly (a deliberate action, with confirmation).
   - **Reject**, discard the draft.
5. **Rollback**, an item that went live or to the CMS can be reverted to draft with one click.

## Via GENI and on autopilot

The Authority Engine is a real GENI skill. So you can also just **ask it in chat** ("write a draft about X" or "build a topical map about Y"), and, more importantly, **schedule it via Automations**. Create a scheduled task on the Tasks page (or use a ready-made template like "Weekly content draft") and GENI writes new drafts on your cadence. They always land in the approval queue, publishing stays your choice.

## Safe by design

- **Draft-first**, GENI never touches your site unless you explicitly publish.
- **Mandatory approval**, every publication is a deliberate action. This protects you against Google's policy on unchecked mass AI content.
- **Volume limit**, up to 30 drafts per month, quality over quantity.
- **Activity log**, every writing action is logged.
- **Secure connection**, credentials stored encrypted, revocable any time. WordPress Application Passwords and Webflow tokens grant only the access needed.
