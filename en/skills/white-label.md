# White Label Content

With White Label Content GENI writes in the voice of a brand other than your own, and remembers that brand profile so you do not have to explain it every time.

Available from **Pro**. Built for agencies and freelancers who write for several
brands.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Create content in a brand's style** , social post, email, blog intro, product description or slogan, based on brand name, tone, core values and audience
- **Save a brand profile** so you do not re-enter those details every time

## Example: what you ask, what you get

First the profile:

```
Save the brand profile for De Krent Bakery.
Tone: warm, matter-of-fact, no exclamation marks.
Values: craft, baked daily, local.
Audience: families in the neighbourhood, 30 to 55.
```

> Brand profile **De Krent Bakery** saved. From now on you only have to say what
> you want.

Then:

```
Social post for De Krent about the new sourdough
```

> **Social post , De Krent Bakery**
>
> > It took three weeks of trying before we were happy.
> >
> > Our new sourdough proves for 24 hours. That is long, and that is exactly the
> > point: more flavour, a firmer crust, and easier to digest than bread rushed
> > through in two hours.
> >
> > In the shop from Saturday. Once it is gone it is gone, we are baking forty a
> > day for now.
>
> No exclamation marks, and the emphasis sits on craft and baked daily, as the
> profile says.

## Limits

- **It publishes nothing.** You get the text; posting runs through [Social Media Manager](social-media.md), [CMS Publisher](cms-publisher.md) or by hand.
- **One brand profile active at a time.** If you work for several clients, use a [project](../functies/projecten.md) per client to keep the context separate.
- **A brand profile is not a brand guide.** Tone, values and audience steer it; specific word choices and forbidden terms you state alongside.
- **No images.** Generating them is possible with [AI Image Generation](ai-beeldgeneratie.md).
- **No legal checking.** Claims about health, sustainability or pricing are yours to verify.

## Troubleshooting

**It does not sound like the brand.** Add an example of existing copy that does land; that works better than adjectives.

**It uses words the client dislikes.** Put them in the profile as things to avoid, for example "no exclamation marks, never 'unique'".

**The brand profiles bleed into each other.** Use a separate [project](../functies/projecten.md) per client.

**The content does not suit the channel.** Name the type: a social post reads differently from a blog intro or a product description.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use this for client work?**
Yes, that is what it is for. See also the
[agencies page](https://dgenix.com/agencies) for what else comes with it, such as white-label
reports.

**How many brands can I save?**
Several profiles, but one active at a time in a conversation. Projects keep them
apart.

**Can I share a brand profile with a colleague?**
Profiles sit with your own account. For team work a shared project is handier.

**What is the difference with simply asking for a post?**
Without a profile GENI writes in your voice. With one it writes consistently in
the brand's voice, months later too.
