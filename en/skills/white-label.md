# White Label Content

## What does this skill do?

With White Label Content you generate copy fully in the style and identity of a specific brand. Set a brand profile, name, tone, values, audience, and then have the assistant write social posts, emails, newsletters or other content as if it comes from that brand.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro or higher
- **Integrations:** none (prompt-only skill)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in the dashboard
2. Find **White Label Content** and click **Activate**
3. Set your brand profile via the assistant

## Setting a brand profile and generating content

**Save a brand profile:**
> "Save a brand profile for 'De Korst Bakery': tone = warm and artisanal, values = honest, local, fresh, audience = families in Eindhoven"

**Generate content:**
> "Write an Instagram post for De Korst Bakery about our new sourdough bread"

**Other examples:**
- A LinkedIn article in a client's style
- A newsletter for a local sports club
- Product descriptions for a webshop
