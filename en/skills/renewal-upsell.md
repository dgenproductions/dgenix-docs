# Renewal and Upsell

With the Renewal skill GENI looks at your client base and points out who needs attention: contracts running out and clients who have gone quiet.

Available from **Pro**.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Run a renewal scan** across your clients and surface the five most urgent
- **Write a re-engagement message** for a client you want to approach again

The value is in that first step. Losing clients rarely happens with a bang; it
happens because nobody noticed it had been quiet for four months.

## Limits

- **It sends nothing on its own.** You get a draft; sending is yours or through [Gmail](gmail.md), with confirmation.
- **The scan works on what it can see.** Without a CRM connection or recorded client data the analysis is thin.
- **These are signals, not certainties.** A quiet client may simply be busy.
- **Top 5 per scan.** For a full overview run it more often or use your CRM.
- **No amounts or forecasts.** It says who needs attention, not what it is worth.

## Troubleshooting

**The scan yields little.** GENI needs client data. Connect your CRM or record client contacts in your [memory](../functies/geheugen.md) or [workspace](../functies/werkruimte.md).

**The signals are wrong.** Say what counts as needing attention for you; some clients are seasonal and that is not an alarm.

**The message is too salesy.** Ask for a short, genuine check-in rather than an offer. That usually works better with quiet clients.

**The same clients keep coming back.** That is correct while nothing has changed. Follow up or mark them as handled in your workspace.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I run this monthly?**
Yes, as a scheduled task. That is exactly what this skill is for: a short list
once a month instead of never getting round to it. See
[Scheduled tasks](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md).

**Does it work without a CRM?**
To a limited extent. It then uses what is in your memory and workspace.

**What is the difference with the Reputation Engine?**
That asks clients for a review. This skill flags clients at risk of dropping
away. See [Reputation Engine](reputation-engine.md).

**Can it make an offer itself?**
It writes the message; what you offer and at what price is yours to decide.
