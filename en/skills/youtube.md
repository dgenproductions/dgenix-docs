# YouTube Channel Manager

## What does this skill do?

The YouTube skill analyses your channel data, generates video ideas, creates upload checklists and shows analytics insights. Connected via Google OAuth so your assistant can fetch live channel data.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** Google account with a YouTube channel (OAuth)

## Connecting Google YouTube

1. Go to **Connectors**
2. Click **YouTube Manager** and then **Connect**
3. Log in with your Google account and grant permission
4. Your channel is active right away

> If you connected YouTube earlier: disconnect and reconnect to activate Analytics permissions.

## What can you do with it?

**Analyse the channel:**
> "Analyse my YouTube channel and show the last 10 videos"

**Generate video ideas:**
> "Generate 10 video ideas for my channel in the fitness niche"

**Make an upload checklist:**
> "Make an upload checklist for my video about home workouts"

**Get video statistics:**
> "What are the stats of video dQw4w9WgXcQ?"

**Analyse comments:**
> "Analyse the comments on my latest video for content opportunities"

**Analytics report:**
> "Give an analytics report for the past 28 days"

**Audience insights:**
> "When is the best time to upload my videos?"
