# Smart Shopping

The Smart Shopping skill helps you quickly find the best products and deals based on your needs.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Compare products on price, quality and reviews
- Recommend the best shop or platform for a specific product
- Create a shopping list based on a recipe or need
- Suggest gifts based on budget and recipient
- Find alternatives for products that are out of stock

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or Pro
- **Integration:** no extra connection needed

---

## How do you activate Smart Shopping?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Smart Shopping
3. The skill is active right away, no extra configuration needed

---

## Example commands

```
Find a good wireless mouse for working from home, budget max €60
```
```
Make a shopping list for pasta bolognese for 4 people
```
```
What's a good gift for my dad (60, loves cooking)? Budget €50
```
