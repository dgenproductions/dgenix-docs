# Meal Planner

## What does this skill do?

The Meal Planner generates a full 7-day weekly menu based on your needs, diet, allergies, cuisine and budget. You get three meals per day (breakfast, lunch, dinner) with prep times and an automatic shopping list.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** none (prompt-only skill)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in the dashboard
2. Find **Meal Planner** and click **Activate**
3. The skill is available right away in the AI assistant

## What can you do with it?

**Generate a weekly menu:**
> "Make a weekly menu for 2 people, vegetarian, no nuts, Mediterranean"

**Create a shopping list:**
> "Generate the shopping list for our weekly menu"

**Other examples:**
- Weekly menu for a family with kids
- Keto diet with a €60/week budget
- High-protein plan for athletes
