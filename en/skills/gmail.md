# Gmail

With the Gmail skill your assistant reads, writes and manages your email via the dashboard or Telegram.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Summarise your inbox and categorise unread messages
- Draft and send emails directly from chat
- Write replies based on context and your style
- Recognise and archive newsletters
- Search email by sender, subject or date

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter, Growth or Pro (included in all plans)
- **Integration:** connect a Google account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

---

## How do you activate Gmail?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Gmail
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on Gmail and complete the Google consent screen
5. After approval Gmail is active right away

---

## Example commands

```
Give me a summary of my unread emails
```
```
Send an email to jan@company.com: "Hi Jan, confirming our meeting tomorrow at 10:00."
```
```
Draft a reply to Sofie's email about the quote, friendly tone
```
