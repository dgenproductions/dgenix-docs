# Meeting Assistant

Let your AI assistant take minutes, capture action items and write summaries after meetings. Give the context or a transcript, the rest is automatic.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Generate minutes from a transcript or description
- Extract action items and assign them to people
- Write a summary for attendees
- Record and archive decisions
- Draft a follow-up email or message

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro
- **Integrations:** none, you provide the transcript or notes directly in chat

---

## How do you use the Meeting Assistant?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Meeting Assistant
3. Start a conversation and send the meeting transcript or your notes
4. Ask for minutes, action items or a summary

No separate connection needed. You paste the transcript in the chat and the assistant processes it directly.

---

## Example commands

```
Here are my notes from this morning's meeting: [paste text]
Turn them into structured minutes with action items.
```
```
Draft a follow-up email for all meeting attendees
```
```
What were the decisions and who is responsible for what?
```
