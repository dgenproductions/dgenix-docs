# Team Assistant

## What does this skill do?

The Team Assistant processes team messages, meeting notes and status updates. Paste text directly or connect Slack for automatic retrieval, the assistant summarises, extracts action items and builds a structured task list.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** Slack (optional, for automatic message retrieval)

## Connecting Slack (optional)

1. Go to **Connectors -> Slack**
2. Click **Connect** and authorise the dGENIX Slack app in your workspace
3. Select the channels the assistant can access

## What can you do with it?

**Summarise a team update:**
> "Summarise these standup notes: [paste text]"

**Extract action items:**
> "Pull all action items and deadlines from these meeting minutes"

**Fetch Slack messages:**
> "Get the messages from the past 24 hours in the #projects channel"

**Other examples:**
- Compile a weekly team summary
- Document decisions from meetings
- Generate a task list per team member
