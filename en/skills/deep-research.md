# Deep Research

With Deep Research GENI splits your question into sub-questions, researches each separately and merges the findings into one report with a source per claim.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Run in-depth research** at two depths: standard (4 sub-questions) or deep (8 sub-questions), with an optional focus

The difference with plain searching is the setup: it first works out *which*
questions need answering, researches them one by one, and only then puts the
findings side by side. That way you do not miss the question you had not thought
of.

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Research whether we should sell our POS integration as a separate product.
Focus: small hospitality businesses in the Netherlands. Depth: deep
```

> **Deep Research , POS integration as a separate product**
> *8 sub-questions researched, 34 sources*
>
> **What I looked into**
> 1. Market size of small hospitality in the Netherlands
> 2. Which POS systems dominate
> 3. Existing integration vendors and their pricing
> 4. What operators say about integrations
> 5. Technical dependencies and API limits
> 6. Pricing models in adjacent markets
> 7. Support load for this type of product
> 8. Legal and payment regulation
>
> **Conclusion**
> There is demand, but not for a standalone integration. Operators buy a POS
> system and expect integrations to be included , three of the four largest
> vendors now ship them as standard.
>
> **The opening that does exist**
> Two POS systems with roughly 18 percent combined market share have no
> accounting integration and refer customers to third parties. That is a
> narrower but real market.
>
> **What to watch**
> - Support is the hidden cost: integrations break on every update from either
>   side
> - Payment data brings extra requirements
>
> **Sources per finding**
> [34 links, grouped per sub-question]

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth and up
- **Connection:** none

## Activating

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **Deep Research**
2. Ask your question, name the depth and optionally a focus

## What it costs

| Depth | Sub-questions | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | 4 | ~3,500 |
| Deep | 8 | ~7,500 |

This is one of the more expensive skills, because each sub-question is searched
and analysed separately. For a quick scan,
[News & Research](nieuws-research.md) at 150 credits is usually enough. See
[The credit system](../hoe-het-werkt/credits.md).

## Limits

- **Public sources.** Paid reports, trade press behind a paywall and internal data stay out of view.
- **It takes a while.** Eight sub-questions means eight search rounds; reckon on several minutes.
- **A conclusion is a conclusion based on what is findable.** Where data is missing, it says so rather than filling the gap.
- **No company-specific figures.** It does not know your revenue, margin or customer data unless you supply them.
- **With too few credits it does not start.** You are told the cost up front.

## Troubleshooting

**The report heads the wrong way.** Give it a focus. Without one it picks the sub-questions itself, and those are broader than you probably want.

**Too expensive for what I need.** Use [News & Research](nieuws-research.md) for a scan, and bring in Deep Research once you know which question is worth it.

**"Not enough credits".** Standard needs around 3,500 and deep around 7,500 credits available up front. Top up or pick the lower depth.

**I want to check the sources.** They sit grouped per sub-question below the report, precisely for that.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference with News & Research?**
[That one](nieuws-research.md) does a single round for 150 credits. Deep Research
devises sub-questions and researches them separately, giving a fuller picture.

**Can I keep the report?**
Yes, ask GENI to put it as a note in your
[Workspace](../functies/werkruimte.md), or have it emailed.

**Can I schedule this?**
Technically yes, but at this price it rarely makes sense. A weekly Deep Research
quickly costs more in credits than a Starter plan.

**Does it work for competitive research?**
Yes, that is one of the strongest uses. Name the competitors explicitly.
