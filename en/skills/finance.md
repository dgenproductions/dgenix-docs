# Personal Finance

## What does this skill do?

The Finance skill analyses your income, fixed and variable costs, debts and savings goals. You enter the data as text and the AI assistant gives concrete, personal financial advice, including a savings plan.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** none (prompt-only skill)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in the dashboard
2. Find **Personal Finance** and click **Activate**
3. The skill is available right away in the AI assistant

## What can you do with it?

**Analyse a financial overview:**
> "Analyse my finances: net income €2,800, rent €950, subscriptions €150, groceries €400. Savings goal: car €8,000"

**Build a savings plan:**
> "Make a savings plan for the next 6 months based on my situation"

**Other examples:**
- Build a debt repayment strategy
- Calculate a holiday budget
- Overview of financial priorities
