# Content Repurposing

## What does this skill do?

Send a YouTube URL, the assistant analyses the video and immediately generates reusable content for 4 platforms: a LinkedIn post, an Instagram caption, a tweet and a short blog summary.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** no extra connections needed

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **Content Repurposing** and click Activate

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Repurpose this video for social media: youtube.com/watch?v=..."
- "Make content from this YouTube clip for LinkedIn and Instagram"

**What the assistant does:**

1. Fetches the transcript of the YouTube video
2. Analyses the core message and highlights
3. Generates 4 platform-specific variants:
   - **LinkedIn**, a professional post with insight
   - **Instagram**, a visual caption with hashtags
   - **X (Twitter)**, a short punchy tweet
   - **Blog**, a short summary (intro + 3 key points)

Everything is saved as a file in your dashboard.
