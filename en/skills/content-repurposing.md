# Content Repurposing

With the Content Repurposing skill GENI turns one piece of content into several platform versions, each in the form that works there.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed.

## What can GENI do with Content Repurposing?

- **Turn one source into several platform variants** in a single request

The point is not copying the same text four times. LinkedIn wants a personal
story, X wants short and sharp, a newsletter wants context and Instagram wants a
hook. Same substance, four different shapes.

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Turn this blog article into content for LinkedIn, X, my newsletter and Instagram
```

> **LinkedIn** , 140 words, personal angle
> "Last week a client called with a question I hear often…"
>
> **X** , 3 separate posts, each under 280 characters
> "Most sites block exactly the AI bots that would cite them. For years."
>
> **Newsletter** , 320 words, with a short intro and a closing line
>
> **Instagram** , a 90-word caption plus 12 hashtags, with a suggestion for the
> visual: a screenshot of the robots.txt line.
>
> Everything is in your Workspace. Shall I publish the LinkedIn version now?

## Limits

- **Publishing does not happen by itself.** You get the texts; posting is yours, or through [LinkedIn](linkedin.md) or the [Social Media Manager](social-media.md).
- **It does not invent facts.** What is not in your source does not appear.
- **No visuals.** At most it suggests what you could show.
- **Quality depends on the source.** A thin article yields thin variants.
- **You stay responsible for what you post.** Check the texts for figures and promises.

## Troubleshooting

**The variants look too similar.** Say per platform what you want ("LinkedIn personal, X sharp") and it differentiates more strongly.

**The tone is off.** Record your writing style in [Settings](../functies/instellingen.md); it then applies everywhere.

**It misses the point of the article.** Name the core message in your request and it builds around that.

**A video source yields little.** Without clear speech there is little to repurpose; check that the video is transcribable.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which sources can I use?**
A blog article, a video URL, or just text you paste into the chat.

Yes, for example turning your latest blog into social posts weekly. See

**What is the difference with the Short Generator?**

[Short Generator](short-generator.md).

**Can it publish straight away?**
Only through a skill that can, such as [LinkedIn](linkedin.md), and always with
confirmation.
