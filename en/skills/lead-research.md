# Lead Research and Outreach

With the Lead Research skill GENI finds out what a company does and writes a first message based on it that actually says something.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed, unless you want to send the
message straight away.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Research a company** and summarise the findings
- **Write an outreach message** based on that, tailored to what it found

The difference with a template is that the message refers to something. "I saw
you just opened a second location" simply opens better than "Dear business
owner".

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Find out what Bakkerij De Krent does and write a first email
```

> **Bakkerij De Krent, Amsterdam**
>
> - Artisan bakery, two locations, since 2019
> - Opened a second shop in Amsterdam-Noord in June
> - Also supplies hospitality; the site has a separate business offering
> - Active on Instagram, over 4,000 followers, posts almost daily
>
> **Draft email**
>
> Subject: Question about your hospitality supply
>
> Hi De Krent team, I saw you have been in Noord since June and that you supply
> hospitality businesses. […]
>
> Shall I send it? That costs 50 credits extra.

2. Give a company name or website and say what you want to know

## Limits

- **Public information only.** What is not on the internet, it does not find.
- **No finding email addresses.** It writes the message; you supply the address.
- **No scraping lists.** One company per request, not a bulk prospect list.
- **Sending asks for confirmation**, and only works if Gmail is connected.
- **Check the facts before you send.** Public sources lag; a wrong fact in a first email costs more than no email.
- **GDPR compliance is yours.** Cold outreach to businesses is allowed, but stay within the rules on personal data.

## Troubleshooting

**Little is found.** Small companies with a thin website yield little. Supply context yourself and GENI builds on it.

**The message is too generic.** Say what you offer and why you are approaching this company specifically; the angle gets sharper.

**The information is wrong.** Public sources are not always current. Verify the facts, especially figures and names.

**Sending fails.** Gmail is not connected, or the address contains a typo.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have a whole list researched?**
No, one company at a time. At 750 credits each that is also deliberate: you
focus on leads worth the effort.

**What is the difference with Deep Research?**
[Deep Research](deep-research.md) researches a topic or market broadly. This
skill focuses on one company and delivers a message with it.

**Can it put the lead in my CRM?**
Yes, combined with [HubSpot](hubspot.md) you write the contact straight in.

**Can I schedule this?**
You can, but it is rarely sensible: you want to decide per lead whether it is
worth 750 credits.
