# Lead Research & Outreach

## What does this skill do?

Your assistant researches potential clients and writes ready-to-send outreach. Give a company name or sector, and it searches online, analyses the situation and generates a personalised cold email and LinkedIn DM ready to send.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** Gmail (optional, for direct sending)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **Lead Research & Outreach** and click Activate
3. Connect Gmail if you want to send directly (optional)

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Research Company X and write a cold email for our service"
- "Generate a LinkedIn DM for this type of company in hospitality"
- "Find 5 potential clients in Eindhoven for social media marketing and write outreach messages"

**What the assistant does:**

1. Searches company information online (website, LinkedIn, news)
2. Analyses pain points and opportunities
3. Writes a personalised cold email
4. Writes a matching LinkedIn DM
5. Saves the results as a file in your dashboard
6. Optionally sends the email directly via Gmail
