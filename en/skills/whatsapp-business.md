# WhatsApp Business

Manage your WhatsApp Business conversations via your AI assistant. Read incoming messages, draft replies and handle customer communication automatically, without sitting behind your phone.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Read and summarise incoming WhatsApp messages
- Draft replies based on context
- Send messages to customers or contacts
- Answer FAQs automatically
- Categorise conversations (lead, support, order, etc.)

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** WhatsApp Business API access (Meta Developer account)

---

## How do you activate WhatsApp Business?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on WhatsApp Business
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Enter your WhatsApp Business API credentials (Access Token, Phone Number ID, Business Account ID)
5. The skill is active right away

---

## Example commands

```
Read my latest WhatsApp messages and summarise what's going on

```
