# Weekly Report Generator

## What does this skill do?

Your assistant automatically generates a weekly overview of your work. Based on your Gmail and Google Sheets it makes a concise weekly report, ready for internal use or client communication.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** Gmail (recommended), Google Sheets (recommended)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **Weekly Report Generator** and click Activate
3. Connect Gmail and/or Google Sheets for richer reports (recommended)

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Generate a weekly report for the past 7 days"
- "Make an overview of what was done this week for client Company X"
- "Send me a weekly report every Friday automatically" *(via Workflow Automations)*

**What the assistant does:**

1. Reads relevant emails from Gmail (past 7 days)
2. Pulls data from connected Google Sheets
3. Generates a structured weekly report (markdown)
4. Saves it as a file in your dashboard

**Report content:**
- Activities and projects
- Client contact and updates
- Open actions
