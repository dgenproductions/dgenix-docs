# Google Docs

With the Google Docs skill you have GENI write, read, summarise and update documents without opening a browser. You get the link back straight away.

Available from **Growth**. You need a connected Google account.

## What can GENI do with Google Docs?

- **Read a document** via its URL or document ID
- **Create a new document** with content it writes itself
- **Summarise a long document** down to the key points
- **Edit a section** or propose a change in an existing document

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Write a project proposal for Bakkerij De Krent, two pages
```

> Done: **Project proposal Bakkerij De Krent**
> `docs.google.com/document/d/1a2b3c...`
>
> Structured as: background, approach in three phases, planning, investment and
> next step. I kept your standard payment terms and the formal tone.
>
> Shall I fill in the investment table with amounts?

And when summarising:

```
Summarise the contract in this doc
```

> **The core in five points**
>
> - Term 12 months, automatically renewed for 12 months
> - Notice period 3 months, in writing
> - Annual price indexation, capped at 5 percent
> - Liability limited to the invoice amount
> - Disputes before the Oost-Brabant district court
>
> Watch the automatic renewal; that one is easy to miss.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth and up
- **Connection:** a Google account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

## Activating

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **Google Docs**
2. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** and click **Connect**
3. Complete the Google consent screen
4. Google Docs is usable right away

## Limits

- **Creating and editing ask for confirmation.**
- **GENI does not delete documents.**
- **Formatting stays simple.** Headings, paragraphs and lists yes; columns, boxes and images no.
- **It works on text.** Tables are read, but complex formatting is lost.
- **Only documents your account can reach.**
- **An edit overwrites the section.** For large changes it is better to have a new document created.

## Troubleshooting

**It cannot open the document.** Check that your account has access and that you gave the right link. A link to a folder does not work, only to a document.

**The formatting disappoints.** Ask for the text and do the formatting yourself, or have it keep to a simpler structure.

**An edit lands in the wrong place.** Name the section as precisely as possible, for example by the heading above it.

**Creating costs more credits than expected.** That is correct: 650 credits, because a full text is written. Reading and summarising are far cheaper.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference with Google Drive?**
[Drive](google-drive.md) finds and organises files. Docs writes and edits the
content.

**Can it write in my house style?**
The text follows your knowledge document and settings. The visual house style
you set yourself through a template in Google Docs.

**Can I schedule this?**
Yes, for example a monthly report it stages as a document. See
[Scheduled tasks](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md).

**What is the difference with the Authority Engine?**
That writes SEO articles and publishes to your CMS. Docs is for ordinary
documents. See [Authority Engine](authority-engine.md).
