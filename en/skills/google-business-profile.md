# Google Business Profile

With the Business Profile skill GENI manages your Google listing: analysing and answering reviews, publishing posts and updating your opening hours.

For a local business this is your most important shop window. Available from
**Growth**, with a connected Google account that manages the profile.

## What can GENI do with your business profile?

- **Fetch your profile details**: name, address, phone number and website
- **Analyse your reviews**: average score, sentiment split and what stands out
- **Publish a post**: an update, offer or event
- **Answer a review** with a professional response
- **View and update your opening hours**, including holidays

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
How are my reviews doing?
```

> **4.3 stars from 87 reviews** *(+6 this month)*
>
> | Sentiment | Share |
> |---|---|
> | Positive | 74% |
> | Neutral | 15% |
> | Negative | 11% |
>
> **Recurring in the positive:** fast service and thinking along when problems
> come up.
>
> **Recurring in the negative:** reachability by phone. Four of the last ten
> negative reviews mention that you are hard to reach.
>
> There are **3 unanswered reviews**, one of them a 2-star from last week. Shall
> I draft a response?

That last part is the point: answering reviews counts towards your local
visibility, and the negative ones are exactly the ones that get left.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth and up
- **Connection:** a Google account that manages the business profile
- A **claimed and verified** Google Business Profile

## Activating

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **Google Business Profile**
2. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** and click **Connect**
3. Complete the Google consent screen
4. The profile is usable right away

## Limits

- **Publishing and answering ask for confirmation.** You read the text before it goes public.
- **GENI does not delete reviews or posts.** An unfair review you report to Google yourself.
- **One location per connection.** Several branches are managed separately.
- **Uploading photos is not supported.**
- **Changes may be reviewed by Google** before they appear; that is their process.
- **Nothing works without a claimed profile.** Claim and verify it with Google first.

## Troubleshooting

**Your profile is not found.** It is not claimed or verified, or your account does not manage it.

**A response does not appear.** Google sometimes reviews responses before they go live. Expect a few hours.

**An opening-hours change does not go through.** On suspicious changes Google asks for re-verification. That runs through Google; we cannot speed it up.

**You have several branches.** Name the location in your request.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference with the Reputation Engine?**
This skill works inside your existing profile: analysing, answering, posting.
The [Reputation Engine](reputation-engine.md) goes a step further and actively
asks your customers for a review. Together: bringing new reviews in and
following them up properly.

**Can GENI answer reviews automatically?**
You can schedule it, but a confirmation still applies. A public response on
behalf of your business should not go out unseen.

**Does this help local visibility?**
Answering reviews and posting regularly are signals that count. See also the
[GEO Engine](geo-engine.md) for your broader visibility.

**Does this work for my agency's clients?**
Yes, provided your account manages their profile.
