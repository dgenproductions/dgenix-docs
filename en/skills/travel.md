# Travel Planner

The Travel Planner skill builds complete travel plans, from flight routes to hotels and local tips.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Plan travel routes based on destination, budget and duration
- Compare airport, hotel and transport options
- Build day programmes per city or region
- Suggest local restaurants, sights and tips
- Make a travel-document checklist (visa, insurance, etc.)

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or Pro
- **Integration:** no extra connection needed

---

## How do you activate Travel Planner?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Travel Planner
3. The skill is active right away, no extra configuration needed

---

## Example commands

```
Plan a weekend trip to Barcelona for 2 people, budget €800
```
```
What are the best restaurants in Lisbon for a business dinner?
```
```
Make a 3-day day programme for Tokyo
```
