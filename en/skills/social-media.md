# Social Media Manager

With the Social Media Manager GENI writes a post shaped to the platform, with the character count attached, and stages it to go out at a chosen moment.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed to write; one is needed to
actually post.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Write a post** for LinkedIn, Instagram, X or Facebook, with hashtags and a character count against that platform's limit
- **Schedule a post** at a date and time, once or recurring (daily, weekdays, weekly, monthly)

Every platform has its own limit, and GENI writes towards it:

| Platform | Character limit |
|---|---|
| X | 280 |
| Instagram | 2,200 |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 |
| Facebook | 63,206 |

Write a LinkedIn post about the De Krent go-live

> `#hospitality` `#automation` `#pos`

> Shall I schedule it for Tuesday 09:00? For B2B that is usually the strongest

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth and up
- **Connection:** none to write. To post you need that platform's connection, for example [LinkedIn](linkedin.md) or [Instagram DM](instagram-dm.md)

## Activating

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **Social Media Manager**
2. Ask for a post for a specific platform
3. To have it posted automatically, connect the platform through **Dashboard -> Connectors**

## Limits

- **Writing and posting are two things.** Without a connected platform you get ready-made text you paste yourself.
- **No images.** Generating one is possible with [AI Image Generation](ai-beeldgeneratie.md), but the image is not attached to the post automatically.
- **No statistics.** Reach, likes and replies come from the platform itself.
- **No replying to comments.** The platform skills handle that.
- **A scheduled post counts against your task slots**: Free 0, Starter 1, Growth 5, Pro 10, Business 20.

## Troubleshooting

**The post is too long.** Name the platform when asking, and GENI writes straight to the right limit.

**It sounds like everyone else.** Give an example of your own tone, or put your writing style in your [memory](../functies/geheugen.md).

**The scheduled post was not published.** Without a connected platform it cannot post; only a task with the text is staged.

**The hashtags do not match my market.** Name three you always use and it keeps to those.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does it post to LinkedIn itself?**
Only with the [LinkedIn connection](linkedin.md) active. Without it, it writes
and schedules, and you paste.

**Can I send one message to several platforms?**
Yes, ask for a version per platform. You get separate texts, because 280
characters on X reads differently from 3,000 on LinkedIn.

**What is the difference with Content Repurposing?**
[That one](content-repurposing.md) cuts existing content into several posts.
This skill writes one, on purpose.

**Can I schedule a series?**
Yes, with a recurring frequency, for example every weekday at 09:00.
