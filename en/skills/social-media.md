# Social Media Manager

The Social Media Manager writes and schedules posts for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), Instagram and Facebook, based on your input.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Write posts based on a theme, product or topic
- Adapt per platform (tone, length, hashtags)
- Suggest multiple variants to choose from
- Schedule posts for a specific time

---

## Supported platforms

| Platform | Max. characters | Tone |
|---|---|---|
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | Professional |
| Instagram | 2,200 | Casual + hashtags |
| X (Twitter) | 280 | Concise + punchy |
| Facebook | 63,000 | Informal |

---

## Example commands

```
Write a LinkedIn post about our new AI automation service
```
```
Make an Instagram post for our opening next week, casual tone, 5 hashtags
```
```
Schedule a tweet for tomorrow at 09:00 about productivity tips
```

---

## How do you activate Social Media Manager?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Social Media Manager
3. The skill is active right away, no extra connection needed

Posts are generated by the AI. You publish them yourself, the assistant prepares the text ready to use.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the assistant publish posts automatically?**
No. It writes and schedules posts in your task overview. You publish manually. Direct publishing via the platforms' APIs is planned for a future version.

**Can I have multiple posts written at once?**
Yes. Ask, for example, "Make a weekly plan with 5 LinkedIn posts about AI" and the assistant proposes a full content calendar.
