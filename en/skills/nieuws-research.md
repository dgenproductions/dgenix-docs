# News & Research

With News & Research GENI searches the web for current information and returns a summary with sources, so you do not have to read twenty tabs yourself.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Research a topic** with a choice of angle: news (current reporting), market (market analysis), competitor (competitive research), trends (sector developments) or general (broad overview)

One tool, but the angle decides where it searches and what the answer looks like.

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
What is happening in Dutch hospitality automation? Type: market
```

> **Market , hospitality automation in the Netherlands**
>
> **What is happening**
> Point-of-sale systems are moving towards all-in-one: ordering, payment, stock
> and staff scheduling in one package. The separate-tools approach is
> disappearing from the mid-market.
>
> **What stands out**
> - Staff shortage is the main driver, not cost saving
> - Self-order solutions grow fastest in fast casual, not in restaurants
> - Accounting integrations are now expected as standard, not as an extra
>
> **Sources**
> - Hospitality sector report 2026 , example.com/report
> - Trade magazine, July 2026 article , example.com/article
> - Market overview of POS vendors , example.com/overview
>
> Shall I put a competitive analysis of three vendors alongside this?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **News & Research**
2. Ask your question and optionally name the type of research

## Limits

- **This is a scan, not deep research.** For an extensive report with sub-questions there is [Deep Research](deep-research.md).
- **Sources are publicly reachable.** Paid trade press, reports behind a paywall and internal data do not make it in.
- **Currency has limits.** Something from this morning may not be indexed anywhere yet.
- **Verify figures before you use them.** It names the source precisely so you can.
- **No automatic monitoring.** To keep following something, set it as a [scheduled task](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md).

## Troubleshooting

**The answer is too generic.** Sharpen the question and name the type. "Trends in e-commerce" yields less than "trends in delivery for small web shops, type: trends".

**There are few sources.** For a niche question there is sometimes little public material. Broaden the question or try it in English.

**It covers the wrong market.** Name the country or region; without that it often picks international sources.

**I want this weekly.** Set it as a scheduled task, for example a Monday update on your sector.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference with Deep Research?**
This skill gives one round of research for 150 credits.
[Deep Research](deep-research.md) splits your question into sub-questions and
searches each separately, which yields more but also costs more.

**What is the difference with simply searching in the chat?**
GENI can search anyway. This skill structures it: angle, summary and source list
in a fixed shape.

**Can I schedule this?**
Yes, through [Scheduled tasks](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md). A weekly
sector update in your inbox is the most common form.

**Does it work in English?**
Yes, and for international topics that often yields more sources.
