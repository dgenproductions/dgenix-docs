# News & Research

## What does this skill do?

Your assistant runs autonomous research on any topic. Give a topic or question, and it actively searches online, analyses sources and delivers a structured report with a summary, concrete findings and sources. From current news to market analysis and competitor research.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** no extra connection needed

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **News & Research** and click Activate
3. Done, no extra setup required

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Research the trends in AI automation for SMBs"
- "What's the latest news about OpenAI?"
- "Analyse the market for e-commerce software in the Netherlands"
- "Research competitors for my sector: HR software"
- "Give an overview of developments in renewable energy 2026"

**Focus types:**

| Focus | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| `news` | Recent reports and developments from the past weeks |
| `market` | Market size, growth figures, key players |
| `competitor` | Positioning, strengths/weaknesses of competitors |
| `trends` | Emerging developments and future direction |
| `general` | Broad overview: background + current state |

**Report structure:**

1. **Summary**, the core of the topic in 3-5 sentences
2. **Key findings**, 4-6 concrete facts and figures
3. **Sources**, consulted sources with descriptions
4. **Insights & implications**, 2-3 concrete takeaways
