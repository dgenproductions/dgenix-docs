# Proposal Generator

With the Proposal Generator GENI writes a complete project proposal based on what you tell it about the job.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed.

## What can GENI do with it?

- **Write a proposal** with the usual structure: situation, approach, planning, investment and next step

It uses your knowledge document and memory along the way, so your company name,
payment terms and writing style are already in there without you saying so.

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Make a proposal for Bakkerij De Krent: new website plus integration with their
point-of-sale system, budget around 12,000
```

> **Proposal , Website and POS integration, Bakkerij De Krent**
>
> **Situation.** Two locations, growing hospitality supply, but orders currently
> come in by phone and email.
>
> **Approach in three phases**
> 1. Website with a business ordering portal , 4 weeks
> 2. Integration with the POS system , 3 weeks
> 3. Handover and training , 1 week
>
> **Investment:** €11,800 excluding VAT, in three instalments
> **Lead time:** 8 weeks after approval
> **Valid until:** 4 September 2026
>
> I kept your standard 30-day payment term. Shall I stage it as a document?

## Limits

- **It does not calculate prices.** It takes over the amounts you name; it does not decide what something is worth.
- **No legal text.** Terms and contract clauses belong with your lawyer, not here.
- **No layout or house style.** You get text; the design you do in your own template.
- **Check the figures before you send.** A proposal is an offer; you can be held to what is in it.
- **One proposal per request.**

## Troubleshooting

**The proposal is too generic.** Give more about the client's situation: what is going wrong now, and why they are asking you. That is what the first section builds on.

**The price breakdown is off.** Name the amounts per phase rather than one total, and it will not split them itself.

**The tone does not fit.** Record your writing style in [Settings](../functies/instellingen.md); it then applies to every proposal.

**There is a validity date you did not want.** Say what you use; by default it applies a common term.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can it stage it as a document?**
Yes, with [Google Docs](google-docs.md) connected it writes the proposal
straight in and returns the link.

**Does it use my earlier proposals?**
Only if you put them in your [knowledge base](../functies/kennisbank.md). Then it
follows that structure.

**Can I combine it with research?**
Yes: [Lead Research](lead-research.md) first for context, then the proposal.

**What is the difference with Client Onboarding?**
This is the proposal *before* the job. [Client Onboarding](client-onboarding.md)
arranges the start *after* approval.
