# Proposal Generator

## What does this skill do?

Your assistant writes a complete project proposal in one command. Give the client name, service and context, and it generates a professional document ready to send, optionally uploaded to Google Drive.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** Google Drive (optional, to save in a client folder)

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **Proposal Generator** and click Activate
3. Connect Google Drive for automatic uploading (optional)

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Write a proposal for Company X for a social media package of €299/month"
- "Generate a project proposal for a website redesign, client has a €5,000 budget"
- "Make a proposal for our AI workflow service for a hospitality business"

**What the assistant does:**

1. Analyses the context and client need
2. Writes a structured proposal (markdown)
3. Saves it as a file in your dashboard
4. Optionally uploads to Google Drive

**Proposal structure:**
Situation -> Solution -> Approach -> Timeline -> Investment -> Next step
