# Invoice Generator

## What does this skill do?

The Invoice Generator creates professional invoices based on client details and line items. The invoice is fully formatted, including VAT calculation. Optionally you save the invoice straight to Google Drive.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Pro or higher
- **Integrations:** Google Drive (optional, only for automatic saving)

## Connecting Google Drive (optional)

1. Go to **Connectors -> Google Drive**
2. Click **Connect** and log in with your Google account
3. Grant permission, done

## What can you do with it?

**Create an invoice:**
> "Make an invoice for client Jan de Vries, Hoofdstraat 1 Amsterdam, for: Web design €1,500, Copywriting €500, VAT 21%"

**Save the invoice to Drive:**
> "Save the invoice to Google Drive"

**Other examples:**
- Invoice for freelance services
- Automatically generate an invoice number
- Invoice in EUR or another currency
