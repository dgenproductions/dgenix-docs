# SEO Blog Writer

With the SEO Blog Writer you have GENI write a blog article built around keywords: with a heading structure, a logical build-up and a meta title and description.

Available from **Growth**. No connection needed.

## What can GENI do with it?

This is a **prompt skill**: there are no external tools underneath. GENI writes
itself, guided by instructions tuned to SEO structure. Concretely:

- Write an article around a keyword, with a logical heading structure
- Generate a meta title and meta description within the right length
- Build the text the way search engines and AI answers read it: answer first, then the detail

That last part is where most articles fail. A piece that only answers in
paragraph six rarely gets cited.

## Example: what you ask, what you get

```
Write a blog about heat pump subsidies for homeowners
```

> **Title:** Heat pump subsidy 2026: how much do you get and how do you apply?
>
> **Meta description** (154 characters): See how much heat pump subsidy you get
> in 2026, which conditions apply and how to arrange the application in four
> steps.
>
> **Structure**
> - How much subsidy do you get? *(answer in the first paragraph)*
> - Conditions you have to meet
> - The application in four steps
> - Common mistakes
> - Frequently asked questions
>
> **Length:** 980 words. Shall I write it to your Workspace?

## Limits

- **No keyword research.** It writes around the keyword you give; volumes and competition come from the [SEO Engine](seo-engine.md) or [Search Console](google-search-console.md).
- **No publishing.** You get the text; posting is yours, or through the [Authority Engine](authority-engine.md).
- **No current facts.** Figures, amounts and schemes are yours to verify; those change faster than a model keeps up.
- **No visuals.** You add images yourself.
- **One article per request**, not a content plan. For a coherent cluster the [Authority Engine](authority-engine.md) is the one.

## Troubleshooting

**The article is too generic.** Give your audience and angle ("for homeowners with a house built before 1990") and it gets more specific.

**The tone does not match your brand.** Record your writing style in [Settings](../functies/instellingen.md).

**It contains figures you cannot verify.** Remove them or replace them with your own data. Do not publish amounts you have not checked.

**The piece is too short or too long.** State the word count you want in your request.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the difference with the Authority Engine?**
This skill writes one article on request. The
[Authority Engine](authority-engine.md) builds a content plan, measures your
draft back against what currently ranks on Google and publishes to your CMS with
approval. That one sits in Pro.

**Can I schedule this?**
Yes, for example a draft every week. See
[Scheduled tasks](../handleiding/geplande-taken.md).

**Will it be recognised as AI text?**
Google does not penalise AI use, but it does penalise unchecked mass production.
Read it back, add your own experience and verify the facts; that is the
difference that counts.

**Can it write in Dutch?**
Yes, name the language in your request.
