# SEO Blog Writer

## What does this skill do?

Your assistant writes full, SEO-optimised blog articles based on a keyword or topic. With headings, internal links, a meta title and meta description, ready to publish.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth+
- **Integrations:** no extra connections needed

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Skills** in your dashboard
2. Find **SEO Blog Writer** and click Activate

## What can you do with it?

**Example commands:**

- "Write a blog article about 'using AI as a freelancer', 800 words"
- "Create SEO copy for the keyword 'SMB automation'"
- "Rewrite this article for better SEO: [paste text]"
- "Generate a meta title and description for this page"

**What the assistant does:**

1. Analyses the keyword and determines the search intent
2. Writes an article with an H1/H2/H3 structure
3. Adds a meta title (max 60 chars) and meta description (max 155 chars)
4. Suggests internal links and related keywords

## Credit cost

| Action | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Write a blog article (per article) | ~50-150 cr |
| Generate meta title + description | ~10 cr |
