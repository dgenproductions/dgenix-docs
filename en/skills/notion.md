# Notion

Manage your Notion workspace via chat. Create pages, update existing content and search your notes, without opening Notion yourself.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Create new pages in your workspace
- Read and summarise existing pages
- Update content based on a command
- Find specific pages by title or content
- Save meeting notes or jottings directly

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** Notion OAuth connection (one-time setup)

---

## How do you activate Notion?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Notion
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Click **"Connect"** on Notion
5. Log in with your Notion account and grant access
6. The skill is active right after connecting

---

## Example commands

```
Create a new page: "Meeting 14 April" with the following points...
```
```
Find the page about our marketing plan and summarise it
```
```
Add to my tasks page: "Send quote to Jansen, deadline Friday"
```
