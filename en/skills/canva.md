# Canva

With the Canva skill GENI finds your designs, auto-fills brand templates and exports the result, without you opening the editor.

> **Coming soon.** The skill is built but waiting on approval from Canva's
> Developer API programme. Once it lands it appears in your marketplace; there is
> nothing to prepare.

Available from **Starter**.

## What can GENI do with Canva?

- **Search your existing designs** by name or type
- **List your brand templates**
- **Create a blank design** in a given format
- **Auto-fill a brand template** with text and images
- **Export a design** as PNG, PDF or MP4

That fourth one is where the gain is: one template plus varying text produces a
series of visuals that all look the same.

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Starter and up
- **Connection:** a Canva account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**, once the connection is released

## Activating (once available)

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills** and activate **Canva**
2. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors** and click **Connect**
3. Sign in to Canva and grant access

## Limits

- **GENI does not design.** It fills your templates; the design itself you make in Canva.
- **Existing designs are not edited**, only new ones created or templates filled.
- **Brand templates may require a paid Canva plan.**
- **Designs are never deleted.**
- **Exporting a heavy design takes longer**; GENI waits and reports back once the file is ready.

## Troubleshooting

**Canva is not in your marketplace.** The connection has not been released yet; see the notice at the top of this page.

**There are no brand templates.** Those sit in Canva's paid plans. Without templates you can still create and export blank designs.

**An export seems to hang.** Canva processes in the background; a heavy design takes longer.

**The filled text overflows the frame.** The template sets the space. Shorten the text or adjust the template in Canva.

## Frequently asked questions

**When will the connection be available?**
That depends on Canva's approval. Once it is through it appears by itself.

**What is the difference with AI image generation?**
Image generation creates a new picture from a description. Canva works with your
own templates and brand style. See
[AI image generation](ai-beeldgeneratie.md).

**Can it lay out a whole campaign?**
It can fill a series of designs from one template. The design of that template
you make yourself.

**Can I schedule this?**
Yes, once the connection is live, for example a monthly series of posts from
your current offers.
