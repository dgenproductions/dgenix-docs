# Canva

## What does this skill do?

Create and export Canva designs straight from your AI assistant. Search existing designs, create blank designs or auto-fill brand templates, and export the result as PNG, PDF, MP4 or GIF.

## Requirements

- Plan: **Growth** or higher
- Integrations: connect a Canva account via **Dashboard -> Connectors**

> The Canva skill is currently in preparation (coming soon). As soon as the Canva developer API is available, the connection is activated.

## How do you activate the skill?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
2. Click **Connect** on Canva and log in with your Canva account
3. Grant the requested permissions
4. Go to **Skills** and activate **Canva**
5. The skill is available right away via the web assistant

## What can you do with it?

- Auto-fill brand templates with your text and images
- Create blank designs for social media, presentations or documents
- Search and reuse existing designs
- Export designs as PNG, PDF, MP4 or GIF
- Get an edit link to open a design directly in Canva

**Example commands:**

- "Make a LinkedIn banner for dGENIX with the text 'AI that works for you'"
- "Use my brand template and fill the heading with our new slogan"
- "Export design ABC123 as PDF"
- "Which designs do I have in Canva?"
