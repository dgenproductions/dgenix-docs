# Stripe Insights

Query revenue data, payment information and customer details from your Stripe account, straight from a chat message. No dashboard to open, no reports to export.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Get a daily or weekly revenue overview
- Identify and follow up failed payments
- Fetch customer information by email or ID
- Monitor MRR, churn and active subscriptions
- Look up specific transactions

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** Stripe Restricted API Key (read-only)

---

## How do you activate Stripe Insights?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Stripe Insights
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Create a **Restricted Key** with read rights in your Stripe Dashboard
5. Enter the API key in dGENIX
6. The skill is active right away

---
