# Daily Planner & Life Assistant

The Daily Planner gives your AI assistant insight into your day, based on your calendar, tasks and personal goals.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Build a daily overview (appointments + tasks combined)
- Prioritise tasks by urgency and impact
- Suggest a day structure based on your work pattern
- Send reminders for important moments

If Google Calendar is connected, your appointments are automatically included in the overview.

---

## Example commands

```
What's on my calendar today?
```
```
Prioritise my tasks for this week
```
```
Plan my morning: I want two hours of focus work before my 2pm meeting
```
```
Suggest a day schedule based on my open tasks
```

---

## How do you activate Daily Planner?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on Daily Planner & Life Assistant
3. Active right away, no extra connection required

**Optional:** connect Google Calendar for a more complete daily overview.
[Google Calendar connector](../connectors/google-calendar.md)
