# CMS Publisher

Publish blog articles and pages straight to WordPress or Webflow via a chat message. Your assistant formats the content, adds metadata and gets everything ready to publish.

---

## What can your assistant do?

- Publish blog articles to WordPress (as draft or live)
- Create pages in Webflow
- Fill in SEO title, meta description and slug automatically
- Save content as a draft for review
- Update existing posts

---

## Requirements

- **Plan:** Growth or higher
- **Integrations:** WordPress (Application Password) or Webflow (API Token + Collection ID)

---

## How do you activate CMS Publisher?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Skills**
2. Click **"Activate"** on CMS Publisher
3. Go to **Dashboard -> Connectors**
4. Pick your platform: WordPress or Webflow
5. Enter your credentials (see below)
6. The skill is active right away

**WordPress:** create an Application Password via *Users -> Profile -> Application Passwords* in your WordPress admin.

**Webflow:** generate an API Token in your Webflow project settings. Also add your Collection ID.
