# Plans & Pricing

dGENIX runs on a subscription. Every plan gives you your own AI assistant (GENI), access to the Skills Marketplace and a monthly bundle of credits. You start free and scale up when you are ready. No contracts, no hidden costs, cancel any time.

All prices exclude VAT. Pay yearly and get a 20% discount.

## Growth Engines subscription (optional add-on)

Using the engines heavily? The SEO, GEO, Authority and backlink tools are credit-heavy (a GEO Share-of-Voice audit, for instance, costs a fair amount of credits). The **Growth Engines subscription** gives you a separate **engine-credit wallet** that is topped up every month and is used *first* for engine actions. That way you don't spend your regular credits (for your assistant and skills) on heavy audits.

| Tier | Price/month | Engine credits/month | For whom |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lite** | €19 | 60,000 | Small business, 1-2 audits per month |
| **Start** | €39 | 150,000 | 1 site, monthly monitoring + backlink building |
| **Pro** | €99 | 500,000 | 2-3 sites, weekly monitoring, GEO Share-of-Voice |
| **Scale** | €199 | 1,500,000 | Agency or many sites |

- **Optional**, without the add-on the engines simply run on your plan credits.
- **Wallet-first**, engine actions use your engine credits first, then your plan credits.
- **Restricted**, engine credits work only for the engines (which is why they cost less per credit than a regular credit pack).
- 20% off yearly. Activate or change it via **Dashboard -> Account -> Subscription**.

## Which plan should I pick?

- **Free**, try it at no cost.
- **Starter**, moderate use: inbox triage, daily planning, the occasional task.
- **Growth**, daily use, generating leads, writing content, measuring your growth with the SEO Engine.
- **Pro**, you want the heavy engines (GEO/Authority/Reputation/AI Content), Workflow Builder or your own Telegram bot.
- **Business**, high volume.
- **Custom**, a team, SLA requirements or white label.

## Credits, upgrading and cancelling

Credits are the unit of account in dGENIX and are topped up each month on your billing date. See [The credit system](../hoe-het-werkt/credits.md). Running low? [Buy a credit pack](credits-bijkopen.md).

Upgrade, downgrade and cancel any time via **Dashboard -> Account -> Subscription**. An upgrade takes effect immediately, a downgrade at your next period. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

See current rates at [dgenix.com/pricing](https://dgenix.com/pricing).
