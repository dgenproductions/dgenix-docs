# Buy extra credits

Out of your monthly bundle but want to keep going? Buy a one-time credit pack, active immediately, no subscription. Available from the Starter plan (Free has a hard limit of 1,000 credits).

All prices exclude VAT.

---

## Available packs

| Pack | Credits | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **S** | 25,000 | €12 |
| **M** | 60,000 | €27 |
| **L** | 150,000 | €60 |
| **XL** | 350,000 | €130 |
| **XXL** | 750,000 | €260 |

The bigger the pack, the lower the price per credit. Credits are added immediately after payment.

---

## How do you buy a pack?

1. Go to **Dashboard -> Account -> Subscription**
2. Scroll to **"Buy extra credits"**
3. Pick a pack and click **"Buy"**
4. Complete payment via Stripe
5. The credits appear on your account right away

---

## When do you need this?

- You have a large job in one go (a batch of leads or a series of audits)
- Your monthly bundle is used up and renewal is a few days away
- You want to bridge a busy period without upgrading yet

---

## Do purchased credits expire?

No. Purchased credits do **not** expire, they stay valid as long as your account is active. Only your monthly subscription credits expire at the end of your billing period.

---

## Prefer to upgrade?

Buying often? Upgrading to a higher plan is usually cheaper. See the [plans](README.md).
