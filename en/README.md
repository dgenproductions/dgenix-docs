# dGENIX, Documentation

Welcome to the official documentation for **dGENIX**, the platform that gives you a personal AI assistant (GENI) with a modular skills marketplace.

---

## What is dGENIX?

dGENIX gives you a personal AI assistant with real memory, ready for anything. You set it up and send commands from the dashboard at [app.dgenix.com](https://app.dgenix.com):

- **Manage email**, read, clean up and draft replies in Gmail
- **Plan your calendar**, schedule appointments and keep Google Calendar up to date
- **Files**, find, draft and organise documents in Google Drive
- **Create content**, social posts, blogs, images and short video clips
- **Do research**, search and summarise the web and your own sources
- **Automate tasks**, scheduled and recurring jobs that run on their own

GENI remembers what you discussed earlier and learns your preferences. Stacking skills builds workflows, fully automated business processes.

Missing a skill? Request it via [dgenix.com/contact](https://dgenix.com/contact?type=skill) and dGENIX builds it and adds it to the marketplace.

You reach GENI through **the dashboard**. With the free Telegram add-on you are also reachable on the go, activate it via Skills whenever you like.

---

## How does it work?

1. **Create an account** at [app.dgenix.com](https://app.dgenix.com)
2. **Set up your assistant**, give GENI a name and a working style
3. **Connect your tools**, Gmail, Google Calendar, Drive, Sheets and more
4. **Activate skills**, pick what GENI can do in the Skills Marketplace
5. **Get going**, straight from the dashboard, instant results

No technical knowledge needed. Average setup time: 5 minutes.
