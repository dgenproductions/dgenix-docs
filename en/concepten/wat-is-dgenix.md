# What is dGENIX?

dGENIX is a platform that gives you one AI assistant, GENI, that does work inside your own tools: email, calendar, documents, social media and your website.

The difference with a chatbot is the word *does*. A chatbot answers. GENI reads
your inbox, writes the email, books the appointment in your calendar and
publishes the article , in your accounts, with your data.

## Who is GENI?

GENI is the name of your assistant. Not a chat window, but one assistant you
meet everywhere: in the dashboard, in Telegram and through voice. It keeps the
same memories, the same skills and the same conversation history, wherever you
talk to it.

During the intake you give it a name and tell it how to write. Prefer "Max" over
"GENI" in your chat window? That works. In this documentation we call it GENI.

## What is the platform made of?

| Part | What it is |
|---|---|
| **GENI** | Your assistant. You give instructions in plain language, it handles the execution. |
| **Skills** | Capabilities you switch on: email, calendar, images, video, research. |
| **Connectors** | Links to your own accounts, so GENI is allowed to work there. |
| **Growth Tools** | Five engines that measure and improve how visible you are online. |
| **Credits** | Your monthly balance. Every piece of work costs credits, and you see the cost up front. |

Those five interlock. A skill without a connector cannot reach anything; a
connector without a skill only grants access. Exactly how they work together is
in [How it all fits together](hoe-alles-samenwerkt.md).

## What can you do with it?

A few things people do daily, to make it concrete:

```
Summarise my unread mail and put the action items in my calendar
```

```
Research what this company does and draft a first email I can send
```

```
Analyse my website for visibility and give me the three worst problems
```

```
Turn this video into five shorts with a title and hashtags
```

You do not have to learn commands. You write what you want, GENI picks the skill
it needs.

## What you see

Ask for an inbox summary and you do not get a raw mail dump but an ordered
answer:

> **12 unread, 3 need action**
>
> - **Sofie van Dijk** , quote approved, wants to sign this week. *Action: send contract.*
> - **Accountant** , VAT return due before the 25th. *Action: supply figures.*
> - **Jan Bakker** , asks whether Thursday's meeting can move.
>
> The other 9 are newsletters and notifications. Shall I archive those?

For an action that changes something or costs money, GENI asks for confirmation
first. You always see the text before an email goes out.

## Where do you work?

The dashboard at **app.dgenix.com** (or **app.dgenix.nl**) is home base: you
chat there, switch on skills, connect accounts and see your usage.

Telegram is a free extra channel for when you are out. You never need it, and
everything stays in sync: same assistant, same memory, same conversations.

## Limits

Honest about the limits, because it saves disappointment:

- **Not an autonomous employee.** GENI waits for your instruction or for a task
  you scheduled. It does not invent work.
- **Not a replacement for your expertise.** It writes a proposal; you judge
  whether it is right.
- **Nothing irreversible without confirmation.** Sending mail, publishing and
  expensive actions always ask for approval first.
- **No access you did not grant.** Without a connector it reaches nothing, and
  you revoke any connection in one click.
- **Never deletes anything.** No mail, no file, no calendar entry.

## What it costs

You pick a plan with a monthly credit balance. Every piece of work uses credits:
a short chat question very few, a full website audit many. What an action costs
shows on the button before you start it, and if a paid action fails you get the
credits back.

On **Free** you can explore the platform and chat in a limited way. From
**Starter** the connectors and most skills become available, and the heaviest
engines sit in **Growth** and **Pro**.

Full indicative pricing is at [The credit system](../hoe-het-werkt/credits.md),
the plans at [Plans and pricing](../plannen-en-prijzen/README.md).

## Frequently asked questions

**How is this different from ChatGPT?**
A standalone chat model talks; it has no access to your mail, calendar or
website. GENI works inside your own accounts, remembers your situation between
conversations and can run tasks on a schedule without you being there.

**Do I need to be able to code?**
No. You connect accounts in a few clicks and give instructions in plain
language. Average setup time is about five minutes.

**Can GENI reach my data without me knowing?**
No. It only reaches what you explicitly connected, and you see per connector
which permissions you grant. Break the connection and access is gone
immediately.

**Where do I start?**
At [Getting started](../aan-de-slag/README.md): create an account, run the
intake, connect your first tool. If you want to understand the whole first, read
[How it all fits together](hoe-alles-samenwerkt.md).
